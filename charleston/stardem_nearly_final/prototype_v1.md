## 1.  How to use this “app”  

| Feature | What it does | How you get points |
|---------|--------------|-------------------|
| **Micro‑Learning Bites** | 2‑minute “flashcards” that explain a term, law, or local practice (e.g., “What is the Maryland Police Accountability Act 2021?”) | +10 pts per bite completed; streak bonus +5 pts each day you finish a bite |
| **Story‑Quiz Challenges** | After reading a story, answer 3‑question multiple‑choice or true/false mini‑quiz. | +20 pts per perfect quiz; +5 pts for each attempt |
| **Badge Levels** | Collect badges for mastering a theme (e.g., **“Water‑Fee Warrior”** after finishing the water‑rate module) | Badges unlock “quick‑reference cards” you can pull up while reporting |
| **Streaks** | Keep a daily streak of any activity (reading, quizzing, posting a note). | Every 5‑day streak = +30 pts; 30‑day streak = **Gold Reporter** badge |
| **Leaderboard** | See where you rank among the cohort of early‑career reporters. | Friendly competition – the top 3 each month earn a coffee voucher (real‑world reward). |

> **Tip:** Treat each story as a “mission”. Open the story, complete the micro‑learning bite that explains its background, then take the quiz. You’ll finish a “mission” and earn points, plus you’ll already have the context you need for the next beat.

## 2.  Step‑by‑Step Narrative: Getting on the Beat  

1. **Orientation – Know the Terrain**  
   *Open the “Map” section of the app.*  You’ll see the five municipalities (Easton, Oxford, Queen Anne’s, St. Michael’s, Trappe) and the key waterways (Chesapeake Bay, Miles River, Eastern Bay, Choptank River, Marshy Hope Creek). Click any dot to pull up a short geography flashcard (2 min).  

2. **Core Themes – Build Your Knowledge Base**  
   *Select “Health & Safety Themes”* – you’ll see six modules (Crime & Policing, Substance‑Use & Treatment, Environmental‑Health, Regulatory Policy, Human‑Trafficking, Community Well‑Being). Each module contains:  
   * a 2‑min micro‑learning video,  
   * a “Key Stats” card,  
   * a short “Story Bank” of the most relevant articles (see Section 3).  

3. **Daily Mission** – Pick one story from the Story Bank, read it, and do the associated quiz.  

4. **Apply the Knowledge** – Draft a quick “beat‑note” (150‑word summary) that answers:  
   * Who, what, when, where, why?  
   * What are the angles?  
   * Who do I need to talk to next?  

5. **Earn Points → Unlock Interviews** – After you collect 200 pts you unlock a **“Contact‑Card”** that contains email/phone numbers for a key institution (e.g., Talbot County Liquor Board). Use it to set up a real interview – the app logs the outcome as extra points.  

6. **Reflect & Pitch** – At the end of each week, the app prompts you to write a 2‑sentence pitch for a possible story (future‑focus, under‑reported angle). The AI‑coach gives quick feedback; a polished pitch earns a **“Pitch‑Ready”** badge.

## 12.  Putting It All Together – A Mini‑Workflow Example  

1. **Morning** – Open the **Map** → tap **St. Michael’s** → read the *Geography* flashcard (2 min).  
2. **Select Module** – “Environmental‑Health.” Open the **Pedestrian Bridge** micro‑learning (2 min) and quiz (5 pts).  
3. **Story Mission** – Read *“Cost for pedestrian trail bridge connector estimated at 8 million”* (12‑05‑2024). Answer the 3‑question quiz (+20 pts).  
4. **Beat‑Note** – Draft a 150‑word note: “MD 50 bridge could cut cyclist injuries by X %; Talbot residents may see a 10‑minute walk‑time reduction…”  
5. **Contact Card** – Unlock **“Bridge to Health”** badge → get MDOT contact email → send interview request.  
6. **Pitch** – At day’s end, write a two‑sentence pitch: “Will the $8 M MD 50 bridge lower Talbot’s traffic‑injury ER visits? I’ll compare projected injury‑rate data with similar projects in Maryland.” Submit to editor; earn **Pitch‑Ready** badge.
