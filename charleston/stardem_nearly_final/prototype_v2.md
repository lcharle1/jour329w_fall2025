## **Talbot County Health & Public‑Safety Reporter Academy**  
*A micro‑learning, gamified onboarding app for early‑career journalists covering the health and public‑safety beat in Talbot County, Maryland (and its towns: Easton, St. Michael’s, Chestertown, Federalsburg, Oxford, Dent on, etc.).*

## 7️⃣  **Sample Capstone Article (What You’ll Produce)**  

> **Title:** *“From Crime Scene to Care Plan: How Talbot County’s Health & Safety Networks Responded to the Federalsburg Shooting”*  
> **Lead:** A 16‑year‑old was charged as an adult with attempted murder after a Saturday‑night shooting left a 20‑year‑old woman fighting for her life in a Federalsburg parking lot.  
> **Body:**  
> *- The Caroline County Sheriff’s Office released the police report (April 14 2024) showing the incident occurred at 11:45 a.m., with the victim transported to Seaford, DE, for emergency surgery.*  
> *- The incident triggered a review by the Caroline County Police Accountability Board, whose February 2024 annual report notes the department logged **26,000** calls for service last year and only **six** complaints, five of which were exonerated.*  
> *- Meanwhile, Talbot Hospice’s Advanced Healthcare Planning class, held Feb 23 2024, reminded residents to prepare advance directives—an issue the victim’s family later cited as critical during recovery.*  
> *- “We want to ensure victims and their families have access to counseling and medical decision‑making support,” said Lauren Weber, vice‑president of For All Seasons, a nonprofit that also runs a free anti‑human‑trafficking course (Jan 2025). The organization is now coordinating with the sheriff’s department to provide trauma counseling.*  
> *- As the case proceeds, the ACC will decide whether any disciplinary action is warranted for the officers involved, per Maryland’s Police Accountability Act.*  
> **Conclusion:** The shooting underscores how Talbot County’s health, safety, and victim‑services networks must act in concert— from police accountability to hospice advance‑directive education— to protect and serve the community.  

*This article combines three story sources from the dataset, demonstrating the “beat‑integrated” reporting style you’ll master.*
