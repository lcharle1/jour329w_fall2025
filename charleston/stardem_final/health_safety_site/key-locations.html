📍 Key Locations

Important places for health and public safety coverage in the Mid-Shore region

📍 Easton

County: Talbot

County seat and largest town. Home to Easton Volunteer Fire Department, Shore Regional Health facilities, and the upcoming A. James Clark Training Campus. Site of multiple shooting incidents.

📍 Cambridge

County: Dorchester

County seat of Dorchester. Location of UM Shore Medical Center at Dorchester. Site of crime incidents and court proceedings.

📍 Trappe

County: Talbot

Home to Trappe Volunteer Fire Company (the county's oldest firehouse, 1951). Site of shooting incidents. Captain Shannon Pinkens' fishing charter location.

📍 St. Michaels

County: Talbot

Historic waterfront town with active fire department. Tourist destination with active emergency services.

📍 Oxford

County: Talbot

Small town with its own police department. Recently experienced leadership transition in law enforcement.

📍 Denton

County: Caroline

County seat of Caroline County. Key location for regional detention center negotiations.

📍 Centreville

County: Queen Anne's

County seat. Part of regional detention center discussions. Home to Queen Anne's County State's Attorney's Office.

📍 Chestertown

County: Kent

County seat of Kent County. Location of UM Shore Medical Center at Chestertown. Part of Compass Regional Hospice service area.

📍 Queen Anne / Hillsboro

County: Queen Anne's

Home to Queen Anne-Hillsboro Volunteer Fire Company. Site of heroic rescue recognition.

📍 Chesapeake Bay

Region: Mid-Shore

Major waterway for the region. Site of rescue operations by Natural Resources Police using thermal imaging. Location for veteran fishing trips.

📍 Route 50

Region: Eastern Shore

Major highway running through the region. Site of multiple fatal accidents. Key transportation corridor.

📍 Mistletoe Drive, Easton

County: Talbot

Location of the A. James Clark Emergency Services Training Campus under construction.

📍 Route 50 & Idlewild Avenue, Easton

County: Talbot

Location of Clark Comprehensive Breast Center near University of Maryland Shore Medical Center at Easton.

📍 Unionville Road, Trappe

County: Talbot

Site of the December 2024 shooting incident that resulted in a fatality.

📍 Wye Mills

County: Talbot/Queen Anne's

Rural area on county border. Site of animal cruelty case investigation.

📍 Preston

County: Caroline

Small town in Caroline County. Location of residential fire incidents.

📍 Secretary

County: Dorchester

Small community. Location of criminal incidents including drug-related cases.

📍 Bozman

County: Talbot

Rural waterfront area. Site of serious motor vehicle accidents requiring emergency response.

🏛️ Landmarks & Establishments

Specific places mentioned in health and public safety coverage

Healthcare Facilities

🏥 UM Shore Medical Center at Easton

Location: Easton, Talbot County

Primary hospital for the Mid-Shore region. Trauma center and emergency care. Multiple stories reference patients transported here for treatment.

🏥 UM Shore Medical Center at Dorchester

Location: Cambridge, Dorchester County

Regional hospital serving Dorchester County. Referenced in multiple emergency response and fatal incident stories.

🏥 UM Shore Medical Center at Chestertown

Location: Chestertown, Kent County

Northern Eastern Shore hospital facility. Part of the Compass Regional Hospice service area.

🏥 Clark Comprehensive Breast Center

Location: Route 50 & Idlewild Avenue, Easton

Breast cancer treatment and screening facility serving the five-county Mid-Shore area. Led by Dr. Kathryn Kelley. Made 137 new breast cancer diagnoses and completed over 12,000 imaging studies in 2025.

🏥 Choptank Community Health System (Federalsburg)

Location: Federalsburg, Caroline County

New community health facility under construction. Site of an intentionally-set fire in May 2024 causing $50,000 in damage.

🏥 Pleasant Day Medical Adult Day Care

Location: Trappe area

Day care facility for disabled veterans and adults. Partners with Easton Elks 1622 for veterans fishing trips.

🏥 Chesapeake Bay Sight Foundation

Location: Beach Avenue, Easton

Nonprofit foundation funding eye disease research. Founded by Colleen Sheehan. Supports Johns Hopkins Wilmer Eye Institute research.

Public Safety & Emergency Services

🚒 A. James Clark Emergency Services Training Campus

Location: Mistletoe Drive, Easton

State-of-the-art 7,200 sq ft training facility for firefighters, police, and EMS. Includes burn building, classroom building, and extrication training pads. Visited by Gov. Wes Moore in April 2025.

🚒 Trappe Volunteer Fire Company

Location: Trappe, Talbot County

Talbot County's oldest firehouse (built 1951). Raising funds for $6 million reconstruction project. Current structure has "questionable" conditions.

🚒 Tilghman Island Volunteer Fire Company

Location: Tilghman Island, Talbot County

Island-based fire department. Assisted in kayaker rescue using rescue boat directed by NRP thermal imaging aircraft.

🚒 Taylors Island Volunteer Fire Company

Location: Taylors Island, Dorchester County

Responded to Slaughter Creek Marina boat fire in July 2025. Controlled $75,000 blaze in about 20 minutes.

🚔 Talbot County Detention Center

Location: Easton, Talbot County

County jail facility. Referenced in multiple criminal cases including holding suspects awaiting trial.

🚔 Proposed Regional Detention Center

Location: 700 Flat Iron Square Road, Church Hill

Former Eastern Pre-Release Unit site. Proposed 388-bed facility to house Caroline, Queen Anne's, and Kent county prisoners. Agreement under negotiation.

🗺️ Coverage Area Overview

The Star Democrat's Health and Public Safety beat covers the Mid-Shore region of Maryland's Eastern Shore, consisting of five counties:

  • Talbot County - Primary coverage area (Easton is the county seat)
  • Dorchester County - Cambridge and surrounding areas
  • Caroline County - Denton and rural communities
  • Queen Anne's County - Centreville and bay-area communities
  • Kent County - Chestertown and northern Eastern Shore

This rural region shares resources for emergency services, healthcare, and criminal justice, making regional cooperation a key theme in coverage.
