Geography & Locations

Talbot County, on Maryland's Eastern Shore, is bounded by water—the Chesapeake Bay to the west, the Choptank River to the south, and smaller tributaries like Miles River and Eastern Bay. The county includes five municipalities: Easton (the county seat), Oxford, Queen Anne, St. Michaels, and Trappe. Its rural landscape, tidal waterways, and agricultural character strongly influence public-safety and health issues:

  • Water rescues — Kayakers, boaters, and swimmers regularly need assistance in areas like Broad Creek, Tilghman Island, and Miles River.
  • Volunteer fire challenges — Long travel distances to state-level trainings and the need for local infrastructure such as the A. James Clark Emergency Services Training Campus in Easton.
  • Heat-related health — Rural and waterfront communities may lack air conditioning; county health departments issue warnings and open cooling centers.
  • EMS access — Shore Regional Health in Easton is the primary hospital; some trauma patients are flown to Baltimore's R Adams Cowley Shock Trauma Center.
